## C. Final shortlist (all Q1, Chemistry–Analytical; all with verified medians ≤ 100 d)

| Criterion | **Analytica Chimica Acta** | Talanta | Analytical Chemistry (ACS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 JIF | 6.1 | **6.7** | **7.3** |
| JCR quartile | Q1 (23/110) | Q1 | Q1 |
| E: median sub→acc, all research papers (n≈1000) | **62 d** | 69 d | 95 d |
| E: share ≤ 60 d / ≤ 100 d | **48.9 % / 87.2 %** | 38.9 % / 76.6 % | 15.6 % / 56.1 % |
| E: chemometrics-subset median (share ≤ 100 d) | 73 d (77.1 %), n = 118 | **68.5 d (79.8 %)**, n = 168 | not measured |
| O: official metric | NF (403) | NF | 90.2 d to accept (ACS metrics, relayed) |
| Method-focused share of recent chemometrics titles | **≈ 15 %** (19/129) | ≈ 6 % (11/173) | not measured |
| Editorial emphasis | **fundamental methodological developments incl. chemometrics; discourages application papers** | all branches of pure & applied analytical chemistry | broad analytical significance; high bar |
| Risk of exceeding 100 days | lowest overall | low–moderate | **high (≈ 44 % > 100 d)** |

**Decision.** Analytical Chemistry is excluded by the 100-day rule (only 56 % of papers accepted within 100 days). ACA and Talanta are both scientifically acceptable. Talanta has the higher JIF (+0.6) and a 4.5-day faster chemometrics-subset median; ACA has the faster overall timeline and a markedly stronger record of publishing pure chemometric methodology. Because scientific fit is a primary constraint and the manuscript is a methodology paper without new measurements, **ACA is selected; Talanta is the fallback**.
