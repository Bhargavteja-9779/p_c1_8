Supplementary Note 2: Pre-specification addendum for B-RPM

170

171

Written 2026-11-21 ~21:00 UTC (session 3; committed in git commit “Add B-RPM …”),

172

173

Rationale

175

Version-1 results and review cycle 1 showed that (i) no pilot-only forecaster beat the no-

176

improvement rule (FLAT) on the registered level endpoint, (ii) a transferred empirical slope

177

(SLOPE-T) predicted gains well within the soil family but failed when transferred across

178

application families, and (iii) RPM with a transferred alignment exponent failed through

179

noise-floor collapse. The observability argument (manuscript Proposition 1) implies that the

180

gain beyond the pilot depends on target power in modes the pilot cannot resolve, which must

181

come from prior knowledge. B-RPM makes that prior explicit, transfers it at the level of

182

structural parameters (alignment exponent r and noise fraction eta) that the deterministic-

183

equivalent theory combines with the task’s own unlabelled eigen-spectrum, and propagates

184

prior and pilot uncertainty into a posterior-predictive forecast.
